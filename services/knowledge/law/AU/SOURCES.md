# Australia (AU) — advertising-law knowledge base

This folder is the backend index for campaigns whose market is Australia. New Zealand, the United Kingdom and the United States have no index yet, so a campaign in those markets is not checked against Australian law.

The PDFs in `sources/` are the official files, kept whole. The files in `text/` are the full text of those PDFs, one page after another. Nothing in `text/` is a shortened extract.

Sourced from the Federal Register of Legislation at 23 October 2026. For the latest information on Australian Government law please go to https://www.legislation.gov.au. Legislation Register content is Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, except the Commonwealth Coat of Arms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
