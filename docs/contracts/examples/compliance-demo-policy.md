# FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — not legislation

This document exists only to test the uploaded-document comparison workflow.
It is not legal advice or an authoritative rule source.

## Scope

For this fictional test only, applies to Australian social media advertising for fruit.
Test version 1, effective 19 October 2026.

## Section 2 — Product claims

Advertisements must not claim that fruit cures disease.

## Section 3 — Discounts

Any discount claim must state the offer end date and applicable conditions.

## Section 4 — Evidence

Claims about origin or certification require supporting evidence from the advertiser.
